## What NanoArch does with your data
- NanoArch works on your computer. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising.
- Your projects (plot, rooms, inputs and the model) stay in memory and in the files you choose to save or export
  (`.nnx`, IFC, DXF, PDF, XLSX, CSV, HTML). NanoArch does not upload them anywhere.
- Images and PDFs you import as a draft sketch are read from the file you pick and shown on screen. They are not saved
  inside the project and are not uploaded.
- NanoArch does not collect analytics or crash reports, and does not send any data to the publisher.

## Optional local assistant
If you turn on the brief assistant, NanoArch sends the text you typed to a model server running **on your own computer**
(`localhost`, `127.0.0.1` or `::1`). Any other address is refused before a connection is made. The publisher receives nothing.

## Components from other companies
NanoArch shows its interface with the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime, which belongs to Microsoft and is covered by
Microsoft's own privacy terms. NanoArch does not control what that component does.

## Your choices
Delete the files you saved to remove your projects. Uninstalling NanoArch removes the application; files you saved stay
where you put them.
